## Project Overview

This project presents a statistical analysis of customer behavior and revenue generation for Megaline, a telecommunications company offering two prepaid plans: Surf and Ultimate.

Using data from 500 customers during 2018, the analysis investigates customer usage patterns, compares revenue generated by each plan, and evaluates whether observed differences are statistically significant.

The main objective is to provide data-driven insights to support the company's advertising budget allocation and commercial strategy.

## Business Problem

Megaline wants to determine which prepaid plan generates more revenue: Surf or Ultimate.

The analysis focuses on two business questions:

- Do the Surf and Ultimate plans generate significantly different average monthly revenues?
- Is the average monthly revenue in the NY-NJ region significantly different from that of other regions?

Statistical hypothesis testing is used to investigate these questions.

## Dataset

The project uses five datasets containing information about 500 telecommunications customers and their activities during 2018.

| Dataset | Description |
|---|---|
| `megaline_users.csv` | Customer demographics, location, registration date, and subscription plan |
| `megaline_plans.csv` | Plan pricing, included allowances, and additional usage charges |
| `megaline_calls.csv` | Call records and call duration |
| `megaline_messages.csv` | Text message records |
| `megaline_internet.csv` | Internet session and data usage records |

The datasets are stored in the `data/` directory.

## Analytical Approach

The project follows a structured data analysis workflow.

### 1. Data Collection and Exploration

- Load and inspect the five datasets.
- Examine data structures, data types, and missing values.
- Identify potential inconsistencies and duplicate records.

### 2. Data Preparation and Processing

- Convert date columns to appropriate datetime formats.
- Apply the business rules for call duration rounding.
- Aggregate calls, messages, and internet usage by customer and month.
- Convert monthly internet consumption from megabytes to gigabytes.
- Combine customer activity with subscription plan information.

### 3. Revenue Calculation

Calculate monthly revenue for each customer based on:

- Monthly subscription fees.
- Additional call minutes.
- Additional text messages.
- Additional internet data usage.

The calculations follow the pricing rules and included allowances of each prepaid plan.

### 4. Exploratory and Statistical Analysis

Analyze customer behavior using:

- Descriptive statistics.
- Mean, median, variance, and standard deviation.
- Histograms and boxplots.
- Monthly usage comparisons.
- Revenue distribution analysis.
- Comparative visualizations by subscription plan.

### 5. Statistical Hypothesis Testing

Two statistical hypotheses are investigated:

**Hypothesis 1 — Revenue by subscription plan**

- Null hypothesis (H₀): The average monthly revenue of Surf and Ultimate customers is equal.
- Alternative hypothesis (H₁): The average monthly revenue of Surf and Ultimate customers is different.

**Hypothesis 2 — Revenue by geographic region**

- Null hypothesis (H₀): The average monthly revenue of customers in the NY-NJ region is equal to that of customers in other regions.
- Alternative hypothesis (H₁): The average monthly revenue differs between the two groups.

The analysis uses a significance level of 5% (α = 0.05) and Welch's two-sample t-test to compare independent group means without assuming equal variances.

## Limitations

- The analysis covers a sample of 500 customers during 2018.
- Monthly observations from the same customer may not be statistically independent.
- Statistical differences do not necessarily imply causation.
- Revenue analysis does not account for operating costs, customer acquisition costs, or profit margins.

These limitations should be considered when interpreting the findings and developing business recommendations.
